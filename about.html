Our story

Why we started.

Too many great businesses are invisible online. They do excellent work, their customers love them, and yet their website is outdated, their social pages are quiet, and the people who'd hire them in a heartbeat never find them.

Corter Digital exists to fix that. We started with a simple idea: marketing should be judged by one thing, whether it brings in customers. Not likes for the sake of likes. Not a pretty site nobody visits.

So we built the agency we'd want to hire ourselves. One team that designs the website, builds it, hosts it, and keeps it running. The same team that plans the content, films it, posts it, and watches what works. No handoffs, no mystery, and no disappearing after launch.

How we work

What you can count on.

01

We do the work ourselves.
