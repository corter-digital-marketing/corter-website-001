Business owners ask us this constantly. The answer surprises most of them: **consistency matters more than volume.** A page that posts twice a week, every week, will almost always beat one that posts daily for a month and then disappears.

## Start with a pace you can hold

Two posts a week is a solid floor. It keeps you visible and builds familiarity without burning you out. Once that's automatic, move to three or more, and add video.

## Make video part of the mix

Short-form video drives most reach on today's platforms. It doesn't need to be polished. Behind-the-scenes clips, quick tips and before-and-afters regularly beat stock-looking graphics.

## Get more out of each piece

You don't need a new idea for every post. One good video can become:

- Several short clips for different platforms
- A photo post with a pulled quote
- A story, a caption, and a talking point for next week

## Watch what works

Once a month, look at which posts got the most views, shares and messages. Make more of those. Growth comes from doubling down on what your audience already responds to.

> Showing up reliably is the strategy. Everything else is tuning.
