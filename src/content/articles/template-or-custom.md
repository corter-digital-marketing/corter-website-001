## Choose a template build if

- You need a professional site online quickly
- The site's main job is to explain what you do and get people to contact you
- You want the most affordable way to look established

A good template, customized to your brand, is the right answer for many service businesses. It's fast, it works on phones, and it does the job.

## Choose a custom build if

- Customers need to order, check out, or place bulk orders
- Customers need to book, schedule, or send detailed requests
- You want to manage your own product catalog or database
- You need to stand out in a crowded market with a design nobody else has

Custom builds are designed around how your business actually runs, so the site does work for you instead of just sitting there.

## Still not sure?

Ask one question: **"What should a visitor be able to do on my site?"** If the answer is "learn about us and call," start with a template. If it involves buying, booking, or logging in, go custom.
