If people visit your site but the phone stays quiet, it's almost always one of these five. Each one is fixable.

## 1. It's slow on a phone

Most visitors are on a phone, often on a weak connection. If the site takes more than a few seconds to load, many leave before they see what you do. Oversized images and heavy page builders are the usual cause.

## 2. The phone number is hard to find

Someone ready to call shouldn't have to look for it. Put the number at the top of every page, make it tappable on mobile, and label the button with what happens next: **"Call now"** or **"Get a quote."**

## 3. It doesn't say what you do in five seconds

Visitors decide almost instantly whether they're in the right place. The headline should say what you do and who it's for, in plain words. "Welcome to our website" wastes the most valuable line on the page.

## 4. Google can't tell what you offer

If pages don't clearly describe your services, Google can't match you to people searching for them. At minimum:

- Give every page a clear, specific title and description
- Set up and complete your Google Business Profile
- Describe each service in the words your customers actually use

## 5. There's no proof

People trust other people. Real reviews, photos of real work, and results you can point to earn more calls than clever copy.

> A website has one job: turn the people who find you into people who contact you. Everything on the page should serve that.
