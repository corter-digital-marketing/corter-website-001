FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly

Which platforms do you manage?

Every plan covers three platforms. We'll help you choose the ones where your customers actually spend their time, rather than spreading you thin everywhere.

Do you create the content, or do I?

We do. We plan, create and post your content. Growth and Scale plans include video content, and you can add an in-person content shoot any time you want fresh photo and video of your business.

How soon will I see results?

Consistency is what compounds. Most of the growth comes from showing up reliably, learning what your audience responds to, and doing more of it. We'll show you exactly how things are trending in your monthly summary.

What's the difference between the plans?

How often you post. Launch is two posts a week, Growth is three a week with video, and Scale is posting every day with video. All three manage three platforms.

What's a clipping campaign?

We take long-form content, like a video, podcast or event, cut it into short clips, and distribute them across multiple accounts. It's the most efficient way to multiply the reach of a single shoot.
