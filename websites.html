FAQ

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Ask us directly

What's the difference between a template and a custom build?

A template build starts from a professionally designed layout that we customize to your brand. It's the fastest, most affordable way to get a great-looking site online. A custom build is designed from scratch around your business and can include things like booking, checkout, or a database.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the build. Template builds are the quickest way online. Custom timelines depend on what the site needs to do. Either way, you'll get a clear timeline on our first call before any work starts.

What do care plans cover?

Every care plan includes hosting, backups, security monitoring and uptime monitoring. Growth adds monthly content edits, performance reporting and speed optimization. Priority adds same-day changes, conversion tweaks and A/B testing.

Can you add a store, booking or payments?

Yes. Custom builds are made for exactly that. We've built online stores with checkout and bulk ordering, booking request forms, product catalogs clients can update themselves, and full databases.

Can I make changes to my site after launch?

Yes. The Growth care plan includes monthly content edits, and Priority includes same-day changes. If you'd rather update things yourself, we can build in an easy way for you to do that.
